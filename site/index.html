Why SkyAlps Virtual

Regional flying, the alpine way.

SkyAlps is Bolzano's own airline. It connects South Tyrol with Europe using one aircraft type and a network built around the seasons: ski traffic in winter, Mediterranean islands in summer. We fly that network in the simulator, with the same timetables.

A real airline's network

34 routes taken from the official SkyAlps 2026/27 timetable: Bolzano to Hamburg, London, Paris and the Greek islands, plus the Rome and Mostar operations.

One aircraft, mastered

An all Dash 8-400 fleet. Short sectors, quick turnarounds and turboprop flying at its best, from 55-minute hops to 2½-hour island runs.

Alpine approaches

Bolzano sits in a narrow valley surrounded by peaks. Every arrival home is a real challenge, and Newsky scores every landing.

Tracked automatically

Newsky's free app logs your flight, files the PIREP and rates it. Your flights earn revenue in a shared economy where every virtual airline competes for passengers.

      FAQ

      Before you push back.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. SkyAlps Virtual is a non-profit hobby project and Newsky is free to use.

      Which simulators can I use?

      Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024, X-Plane 11 and 12. The Newsky app runs on Windows.

      Which aircraft do I need?

      A Dash 8-400 that Newsky recognises as a DH8D. SkyAlps flies nothing else, and neither do we.

      Do I have to fly on VATSIM or IVAO?

      No. Online networks are welcome but never required.

      What happens if my simulator crashes mid-flight?

      Newsky detects interrupted flights. Let a staff member know on Discord and we'll sort it out.

      Are you the real SkyAlps?

      No. SkyAlps Virtual is a flight simulation community. We are not affiliated with SkyAlps, and nothing on this site is a real flight or a real ticket.
